4-Cylinder Engine

A small SolidWorks project I used to sharpen my 3D modelling skills: the moving parts of an inline four-cylinder engine, modelled part by part and put together in a working assembly.

SolidWorksPart modellingAssembly & mates
drag to rotate · scroll to zoom

Why I made it

This was a practice project. I wanted to get faster and more confident in SolidWorks, and an engine is a good exercise: several parts that all have to line up and move together.

What I modelled

#Part
1Piston
2Piston ring
3Crankshaft
4Connecting rod
5Connecting rod cap
6Piston pin

What I practised

  • Revolved and multi-body features for the piston, pin and crankshaft journals.
  • Assembly mates so the four piston groups follow the crankshaft rotation.
  • Working from a reference layout: keeping bores, journals and pins concentric across six separate parts.
